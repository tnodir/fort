# Ask to Connect

Moved out of `AGENTS.md`: read it when working on this area.

Ask to Connect pends a blocked connection till the user's decision: `fort_conf_conn_asked()` asks an unknown (only when logged, else blocked) or alerted App's connection blocked by the "Ask to Connect" Filter Mode (`conf_flags.ask_to_connect`) and, in any Filter Mode, the one blocked by an Ask action (the Program's `app.ask`, a Rule's `ask`/`term_ask`, the filter's `Act(Ask)`: `conn->act.ask`). The ALE callout pends it (`fortpkt_pending.c`: `FwpsPendOperation0()` with its cloned packet, reason `FORT_CONN_REASON_ASK_PENDING`) in a list with the App's path (its referenced name, `fortpsname.c`): an App has up to 9 outbound and 1 inbound pending connections, its connection's repeated packet and the ones over the limit are dropped without logging. The pending connection is logged always (the decided ones by the log flags); the not logged one is blocked by the worker. The master's `PendingConnManager` (`stat/`: a `StatConnManager` variant with its own DB, recreated on the start) gets them from `LogManager`, blocks the timed out ones (`askToConnectSeconds`) by `BLOCKPENDING` and decides them by the user (`decideConn()`: Block, Allow once or Continue by the current conf, by the Paused Connections window `AppPendingConnsWindow`, opened by the program's dialog: its `AppPendingConnSearchModel` keeps the listed ones no longer pending (up to 100), appending the new ones, so the selection stays; the Alert Program window shows the oldest pending connection's program, opened by `TrayIcon` on a new one (`lastConnId()`); its OK goes to the oldest other program with the pending connections): an allowed connection is completed by `ALLOWPENDING` (the flow ids of `BLOCKPENDING`/`REAUTHPENDING`/`ALLOWPENDING` are sorted by the master: the driver finds them by the binary search), which adds its flow id to the driver's sorted allowed flows (`fort_pending_allow_flows()`; a closed flow's id reused by a new pending connection is removed, a not taken one is dropped by the next allowing after `FORT_PENDING_ALLOWED_FLOW_SECONDS`). Its first reauthorization (the completion's or its re-injected packet's) takes the id and allows it once (`FORT_CONN_REASON_ASK_ALLOWED`), its associated flow keeps the decision (`FORT_FLOW_ASK_ALLOWED`) for the next reauthorizations and logs its closing (`FLOW_CLOSED`, not used by the UI yet); else they're blocked (`ASK_BLOCKED`). A saved program's pending connections are reauthorized by its conf, when its action isn't Ask (`PendingConnManager::reauthApps()` on `ConfAppManager::appsDriverUpdated`; the asked program's ones are decided by the user; only the ones asked by the App's data, logged with `FORT_LOG_CONN_APP_ASKED`, of the app deciding the connection: the inherited one, if any), the driver completes the ones of the Apps no longer asked on the conf's change too (`fort_pending_reauth()`; the ones pended by a Rule are decided by the user only). The inbound pending connection can't be allowed once: its flow id is changed by the re-injection. The Paused Connections window's "Allow Process" allows the selected connection's process instead of asking, till its exit or for the menu's time (`allowProcess()` by `ALLOWPROC`): the driver keeps it in the process's node (`fort_pstree_proc_allow()`, so a reused PID isn't allowed; cleared on the master's close), reauthorizes the process's pending connections, inbound too, and allows its asked ones (`FORT_CONN_REASON_ASK_PROC_ALLOWED`).
