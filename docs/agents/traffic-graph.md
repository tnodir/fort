# Traffic graph

Moved out of `AGENTS.md`: read it when working on this area.

The traffic graph (`form/graph/`) has no 3rd-party chart library: `GraphView` (the graph's `IniGraph`, its colors, fonts and format, the right click's tray menu) feeds `StatManager::trafficAdded` into `GraphPlot`, a `QGraphicsView` whose scene items (grid, axes, tick labels, in/out bars, text speed) are laid out in the viewport's pixels; `GraphWindow` is its floating host (the window's flags, opacity and Hide on hover, dragging and the sticky screen edges, the geometry, the speed in the title by `GraphView::speedUpdated()`). It keeps the per-second points itself (a delayed second is merged, not cleared) and is replotted once per second by `GraphView`'s timer, not while hidden; the axes are re-laid out only when the value range, the size or the tick labels change. The text speed is placed by the Speed box's "Position" (`IniGraph::speedPosition()`, `GraphSpeedPosition`: at the top or bottom in a line, at the left or right in two rows of the widest text's width) inside the plot area over the graph, or without "Inside the graph" (`IniGraph::speedInside()`) in a margin of the axis rectangle (`GraphPlot::speedMargins()`, kept as laid out), centered on the graph along the side, separated from the graph by an axis line (below the graph: the X axis line, which the bars stand on; at the right: the right line, also without the ticks); the text alone (the graph hidden) is in the view's center. Between the replots the bars scroll by a device pixel (a second is the "Bar width" option's pixels (5 by default) rounded to the device pixels, the last second at the right edge; the bars are laid out in device pixels too, widths and heights, so they don't jump or gap with a fractional scale like 125%; a second's in and out bars overlap, the smaller one in front, their sum above in the "Total" color; the "Show" options hide the Total, Download and Upload graphs (the Total by default: a hidden one's bars are empty, its line is hidden; the Total line's area is filled by a gradient, without it the Download and Upload ones are, by fill-only `GraphLineItem`s below both lines) and the value range is by the visible ones); the last complete second's bars rise and the value range's change is animated (`GraphAnimation`, ~30 frames per second, not `QVariantAnimation`'s ~60), so the view keeps the default minimal viewport update. The bars are drawn by `GraphBarsItem` as rectangles (`QPainter::drawRects()`): filling a `QPainterPath` of them is ~60× slower. The "Graph type" option (`IniGraph::graphType()`) switches the bars to the Line type: `GraphLineItem`'s monotone cubic curves of 2 device pixels, drawn as antialiased cosmetic lines of a device pixel with offsets (a thick antialiased line is ~10× slower); the lines have no rising points. The bars and the lines are `GraphCachedItem`s: painted to a pixmap aligned to the device pixels on their change, the scrolled ones only draw it (`QGraphicsItem::DeviceCoordinateCache` is slower: it's regenerated on the scroll). Without traffic nothing is scrolled, animated or repainted. The scroll and the animations are switched off by the graph's "Animation" option (`IniGraph::animation()`, `GraphPlot::setAnimated()`): then the graph is repainted once per second.

There are up to 8 graphs (`IniGraph::maxCount`), each a `GraphWindow` of its id with its options in the `.user.ini` group of `IniGraph::groupName()`: `[graphWindow]` for the main graph (id 0, always present, as before the graphs' list), `[graphWindow1]`…`[graphWindow7]` for the extra ones, listed by `graphsWindow/graphIds` (`IniUser::graphWindowIds()`). Their `FormPointer`s are of the same `WindowGraph` code with the graph's id: the main one is `WindowManager`'s form of the code, the extra ones are in `m_graphForms` (`graphFormById()`); only the main one is tracked by the code (`windowOpened()`/`windowClosed()`: the tray's "Traffic Graph" action of the Graphs menu and its hot key, `-c graph` and `-c window graph` show the main graph), all of them emit `graphWindowVisibilityChanged()`, which checks the extra graphs' actions of the tray's Graphs menu (`TrayIcon::updateGraphActions()`, by their names, after the main graph's "Traffic Graph"). The main graph's name is always "Traffic Graph" (`GraphTypes::graphName()`, read-only in `GraphEditDialog`). The Graphs window (`GraphsWindow`, `WindowGraphs`, password-protected, not dockable, opened by the tray's Graphs menu) lists them by `GraphListModel` (its check box shows or closes the graph's window) and adds, edits and removes them: `GraphEditDialog` edits a copy of the `IniUser` by `GraphOptionsArea` (the former Options' Traffic Graph tab) and saves it by `GraphsController::saveGraph()` on OK and on Apply (enabled by `GraphOptionsArea::edited()` and the name's edit); the removal deletes the window and the ini group (`MapSettings::removeGroup()`).
